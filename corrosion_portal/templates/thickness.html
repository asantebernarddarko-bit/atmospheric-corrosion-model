Engineering Interpretation

The assessment examines the effect of atmospheric corrosion on carbon steel thickness over time. Corrosion loss is used to estimate the remaining steel thickness under different environmental scenarios.

The results are model-based estimates and should be interpreted alongside the assumptions and limitations of the corrosion prediction model.

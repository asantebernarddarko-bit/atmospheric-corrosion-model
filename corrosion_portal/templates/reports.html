Research Scope

This research develops a predictive framework for estimating atmospheric corrosion rates and the life-cycle cost of carbon steel structures under different environmental conditions.

The model integrates environmental data, ISO 9223-based corrosion rate estimation, steel thickness assessment, service-life estimation and life-cycle cost analysis.

The study considers Tema and Kumasi under different assumed pollution scenarios.

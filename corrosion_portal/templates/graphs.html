Interpretation of Results

The graphs provide a visual comparison of the influence of environmental exposure, location, steel thickness and replacement threshold on the estimated life-cycle cost of carbon steel structures.

The results are model-based estimates. Pollution deposition values and economic inputs include illustrative assumptions and should not be interpreted as direct field measurements or verified market prices.

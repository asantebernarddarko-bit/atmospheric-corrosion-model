Engineering Interpretation

Service-life estimation is based on predicted atmospheric corrosion rates and the reduction in steel thickness over time.

The assessment considers different initial steel thicknesses and environmental scenarios for Tema and Kumasi.

The predicted remaining thickness does not independently establish structural failure. An engineering minimum allowable thickness must be specified for that assessment.
